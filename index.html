Biography

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Qingfu Zhang. I received my M.Eng. and B.Eng. degrees from Beihang University.

My research focuses on continual learning, multi-objective optimization, and multimodal learning in open and dynamic environments.
